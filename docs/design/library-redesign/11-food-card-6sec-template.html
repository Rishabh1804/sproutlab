Right time, right form. Dairy in food is excellent from around 6 months — dahi (curd), paneer, and cheese give calcium, protein, B12, and healthy fats. The only caution is about milk as the main drink before the first birthday.
Not as the main drink before 12 months — cow's milk is low in iron and hard on a baby's kidneys. Breastmilk or formula stays the main milk to the first birthday.
IF A REACTION HAPPENS — AN EMERGENCY
  • trouble breathing or wheezing
  • swelling of the tongue or throat
  • a hoarse cry
  • going floppy, pale, or very sleepy

A mild reaction to dairy (a little rash alone): stop, watch closely, and call your doctor. Any trouble breathing, tongue or throat swelling, a hoarse cry, or floppiness: call 112 right away and use a prescribed adrenaline auto-injector if you have one.
