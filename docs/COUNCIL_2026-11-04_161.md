## Council brief — BUGS #161: the paracetamol chip's weight-jump check

Convened by Lyra, 2026-11-04, at the Architect's request. Mode-2 delegate positions, all seats, then Cipher. Nothing is built yet; this council decides the shape before a spec is written (canon-cc-013: cite every clinical number).

## The problem
The fever card's paracetamol chip (split/intelligence-illness.js `getParacetamolChip`, `paraWeightPlausible`, ~lines 73–116, 446–483, 630–641) doses 10–15 mg/kg from the latest weight (`getLatestWeight()`, medical.js:1180). Its only weight gate is WHO Anthro's implausible-value flag (weight-for-age z < −6 or > +5). At 14 months the plausible band is about 3.3–16 kg, so a believable typo — 15.9 kg entered for Ziva's real ~9.5 kg — passes and is dosed for 15.9 kg: about a 1.7× overdose, repeatable every 4 hours. The printed weight and date on the chip are today's only defence. BUGS.md #161 (top of docs/BUGS.md) holds the record and Maren's consult summary.

## The proposal on the table (Maren, Mode-2 consult, 2026-11-03)
1. **Threshold:** ask "Is X kg right?" when the change from her previous weight is above the WHO P99 or below the P1 weight-velocity increment for that interval and sex. 2-month table when the two weigh-ins are ≤61 days apart; 3-month table for 62–92 days. Row picked by the latest weigh-in's age (`ageMonthsAt(latest.date)`), never today's.
2. **Pair:** `gvWeightWindow(gvMeasureRows('wt'))` as it stands (#124 — no new row pick); must be the same row the chip doses from, else treat as "no earlier weight".
3. **Edges:** no earlier weight → confirm; earlier weight >92 days back → confirm; future-dated latest already blocked (`baddate`); unknown sex → widest band (max P99, min P1 of girls/boys); past 24 m → use the 22–24 row until a source is chosen (WHO velocity tables stop at 24 m; she turns 2 on 2027-09-04).
4. **Both directions:** a low typo under-dosing → parent re-doses early or stacks.
5. **State:** a new chip state `confirm` (no dose object, no mg, no ml), placed after `implausible`/`nodose`, before `pick`. Never `ok` with a flag — `feLogDosePrompt` pre-fills `chip.dose.lowMl` whenever `state === 'ok'`.
6. **Confirm persistence (PC-11):** store the confirmed value on the growth row, e.g. `wtConfirmed: 15.9`; counts only while `row.wtConfirmed === row.wt`, so an edit undoes it and a new row starts unconfirmed. Never a yes/no, per-episode or global flag. (A schema change to a synced key → Carina.)
7. **Copy:** "Is 15.9 kg right? On 12 Sep {subj} weighed 9.5 kg. That's a bigger jump than children usually make, so check it before giving a dose." Loss and no-comparison variants; buttons "Yes, 15.9 kg is right" / "Fix it in Growth"; always the existing "If {subj} needs medicine now, ask your doctor or chemist for the dose."; after a confirmed loss, "A real drop in weight while {subj} is unwell is worth telling your doctor."
8. **Residual error:** at 14 m a typo up to ~+1.14 kg on 9.5 kg (~12%) passes → top dose ~16.8 mg/kg, ~67 mg/kg/day over 4 doses (normal max 60). Over a 3-month gap ~16%. Maren asks the Architect to accept this against BNFc.
9. **Rejected alternative:** the BUGS draft "≈20% within ≈60 days" — would flag healthy infants (girls 3–5 m P99 is 1894 g in 2 months, ~31%), and is looser than P99 at toddler age.
10. **Maren's own deferrables:** D3 — P1 is a tight floor; illness loss or home-vs-clinic scale difference may trigger confirms (a cited measurement allowance only if confirms get noisy). D4 — the Growth card would still show the typo as "fast gain"; the root fix is the same check at entry (`addGrowthEntry`). D5 — the same typo entered twice passes.

## Citation check (canon-cc-013, Maren V-M-C161-6) — done by Lyra 2026-11-04
- **BNF / TOXBASE (UK):** BNF "Poisoning, emergency treatment": "Toxicity rarely occurs with paracetamol doses between 75–150 mg/kg in any 24-hour period. Doses consistently less than 75 mg/kg in any 24-hour period are …" (search excerpt; bnf.nice.org.uk is UK-only, the page returned 403 here). Corroborated: Sheffield Children's Hospital protocol 3.26 — "Toxbase and BNF advice maximum paracetamol dose to be 75mg/kg/day"; NHS GGC handbook "≥75mg/kg in any 24-hour period". **Verified as the 75 mg/kg/24 h assessment line; full BNF sentence not read in full.**
- **Chiew et al., MJA 2020;212(4) (ANZ):** repeated supratherapeutic ingestion needs assessment at "≥ 10 g or ≥ 200 mg/kg (whichever is less) over a single 24-hour period" and "≥ 12 g or ≥ 300 mg/kg … over a single 48-hour period". Same in RCH Melbourne CPG, plus ">a daily therapeutic dose per day for more than 48 hours" with abdominal pain, nausea or vomiting. **Maren's recalled 150 / 100 mg/kg/day multi-day figures are not in the 2020 guideline — corrected.**
- **Consequence:** the toddler worst case that passes the check (67.2 mg/kg/day on a 2-month gap, 69.5 on a 3-month gap, top of range every 4 h for 24 h) sits below the strictest line (75 mg/kg/24 h) by ~7–11%, and far below the ANZ 200 mg/kg line. Over the 60 mg/kg/day therapeutic maximum. The infant residual (~79 mg/kg/day at girls 3–5 m) is above 75 — unreachable for Ziva, recorded as DEFERRABLE (V-M-C161-11).

## Collective position (what every seat agreed, or no seat contested)

Ceiling = **P99** (the overdose direction stays tight; P99.5 / z+3 rejected — Maren, Kael). Floor = **min(P1, 0)** — infant P1 is positive at 0–9 m, so a short-interval normal gain would misfire (Maren V-M-C161-1).
2. **Registry (PC-9):** ONE cited table, stored with L / M / S / Delta and P1 / P99 per row, from the four WHO files **committed to the repo** with sha256 provenance; a dependency-free build audit re-extracts the columns and recomputes P1/P99 from LMS to ±1 g (Kael C161-5, Carina C161-6). No hand-typed second table. Fetch **boys 3-month** before build (all seats). Placement: data.js beside WHO_GROWTH (Carina's preference) or core.js beside GROWTH_VELOCITY_BANDS (Maren) — Carina's call, both acceptable.
3. **Fail closed (PC-5):** any missing row, age past the table, unknown-sex gap, non-finite interval or non-number weight → `confirm`, never `ok`. Written as `!(change >= floor && change <= ceil) → confirm`. Self-tests in the same commit, including today = 2027-09-05 and an empty table (Kael C161-2, Carina C161-4).
4. **Pairs (PC-10, #124):** the chip tests the `gvWeightWindow` pair (the pair the Growth engines measure — catches the same typo entered twice, D5) **and** the latest vs the immediately previous row when it differs (catches a modest typo among dense weigh-ins); `confirm` if either is out of band (Kael C161-6). The anchor must be the row `getLatestWeight()` returns, compared by date + wt.
5. **Two weights on one date** with different values → `confirm` ("two weights on {date}"), never a silent pick by array order (Carina C161-3).
6. **One shared pure helper** in core.js (e.g. `gvWeightJumpCheck`) — the chip, `_emDocVitals` (diet.js:2382 — the ER doctor card, Ceres C161-2) and, next PR, `addGrowthEntry` all call it (Kael, Carina, Ceres, Maren).

## Dissent and Lyra's proposed resolution
**D-1 — Persistence (Q3).** Maren, Kael, Vela, Ceres endorsed `wtConfirmed` on the growth row (written before Carina's sync analysis). **Carina BLOCKS it (V-CA-C161-1, resurrection):** growthData is one array, replaced whole on any write, and the recovery push merges same-date rows with local-wins — a confirm tapped offline on one phone can resurrect a typo the other parent just fixed, *already confirmed*, and the chip doses with no question. **Lyra proposes Carina's option (a): a separate synced map key** `KEYS.growthConfirm = { 'YYYY-MM-DD': <wt> }` — confirmed only while the stored number equals `row.wt`; object keys merge field-by-field; a confirm never writes the growth array; a stale entry is inert. Armed atomically (KEYS, SYNC_KEYS, SYNC_RENDER_DEPS, sync assign/read cases, persist-defaults decision). Option (b) in-memory is the safe fallback; option (c) on-row only with explicit Architect acceptance of the resurrection path. → **Architect decision A.**

**D-2 — How the parent confirms (Q5).** All seats: "Yes" is never the primary. Vela: confirm by **retyping the weight** in every variant (a frequent tap decays into a reflex — most toddler weigh-ins at 3-monthly clinic visits are > 92 days apart and would trip the no-comparison variant). Maren: retype for the **high** direction only (one-handed cost), Architect's call. Kael: a confirm path must exist in every variant, or clinic-only families never get a dose. **Lyra proposes retype in every variant** (one rule, satisfies Kael, and the no-comparison variant is exactly where the reflex would form); on mismatch: "You typed 9.5 kg; Growth has 15.9 kg" with "Save 9.5 kg" (a growth write from an illness file → Carina write-path consult) or open the row. The handler binds to the **displayed** value (data-arg date + data-arg2 weight); if the row changed since render, nothing is written and the chip asks again (Kael C161-4, Carina). → **Architect decision B.**

**D-3 — Which WHO row (Q1/Q6 detail).** Kael: the interval that **ends** at the latest weigh-in's age (start = round(latest age) − 2 / − 3). Maren (correcting her own consult) and Carina: **start = floor(ageMonthsAt(previous.date))**, clamped 0–22 (2-month) / 0–21 (3-month). They agree for a ~2-month pair and differ for short gaps. **Lyra proposes Maren/Carina's rule** — WHO labels rows by the interval itself, so the interval starts at the earlier weigh-in; written once, tested at month boundaries, and its relation to the midpoint rule of GROWTH_VELOCITY_BANDS (V-CA-282b-1) stated. Cipher to rule.

**D-4 — Interval scaling.** The 2-month band applied to a 1–7-day pair is loose (Kael, Carina note) but no looser than the accepted residual; a linear scale-down without a cited scale-noise floor would fire on real weights. **Keep the 2-month band for ≤ 61 days; a cited, scaled band with a noise floor is its own later spec** (Kael C161-7, Maren C161-12).
