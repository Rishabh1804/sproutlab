Palak & Moong Dal Khichdi
No leafy greens logged in 7 days — iron-rich, pairs dal with spinach for absorption.
20 min · 7m+ · Lunch
More for Ziva2
Breakfast6
Ingredients
Oats7m+2 tbsp
Berriesok10 g, soft
Milk (cooked-in)7m+120 ml
Recipe
Cook oats in milk, stirring, until thick; simmer 2–3 min.
Stir in berries, heat until completely soft.
Smooth for 7–9m; leave soft lumps from 9m.
Dos & Don'ts
Full-fat milk, cooked in (not a drink)
No added sugar or honey
Source: NHS Start4Life — porridge (7–9 months)
