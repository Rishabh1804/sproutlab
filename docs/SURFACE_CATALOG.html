How it keeps
Made on the spot: Make the dry mix at home; it keeps for weeks in a sealed jar. The porridge is made fresh and eaten at once.

Ice-pack bag: Cooked food: 2 hours at most at room temperature, 1 hour on a hot day (above 32 °C), counted from when it finished cooking. With an ice pack in an insulated bag it can last until a meal later the same day, as long as the ice pack is still frozen and the food still feels fridge-cold. If the ice pack has melted, don't give it. WHO · USDA FSIS
