## 1. Problem
The fever card's paracetamol chip (#284) doses 10–15 mg/kg from the latest weight. Its only weight gate is WHO Anthro's implausible-value flag (weight-for-age z < −6 or > +5). At 14 months that band is about 3.3–16 kg, so a believable typo — 15.9 kg for Ziva's real ~9.5 kg — is dosed: about 1.7× her dose, every 4 hours. And the implausible gate itself stops at 24 months (`getInterpolatedWHO` clamps at the 24 m row), so from her second birthday every weight is read against a 2-year-old's chart.

## 4. The weight-jump check
### 4.1 Registry (PR 1)
- One WHO weight-for-age L/M/S table, 0–60 months by month, per sex, from the committed `tab_wfa` files; one `whoWfaZ(wtKg, ageMo, sex)`. Linear interpolation of L, M, S. WHO restricted z beyond ±3 (step in SD2→SD3 units), log form when |L| < 1e-6. `sex` is explicit; never `whoT()` (it falls back to girls).
- The velocity table (P1 / P99 and L, M, S, Delta per row; 2- and 3-month, girls and boys) is its own registry, ≤ 24 m. Knot days named: 61 / 91 / 122 / 183. **Amended at build (#287, V-CA-287-7):** it lands in PR 2 with its first consumer, `gvWeightJumpCheck`.
- A dependency-free build audit re-extracts each committed xlsx (zlib + XML), recomputes P columns from L/M/S (velocity ±1 g; weight-for-age ±0.05 kg) and refuses to pass if a file is missing.
- `WHO_GROWTH`'s weight columns are **bound** to the weight-for-age registry by the build audit: every cell must equal the committed file's P column exactly (WHO's published 0.1 kg value). **Amended at build (#287, Maren V-M-287-1, Carina V-CA-287-1):** bound, not derived — deriving would move displayed percentiles at medical.js's 14 `whoT()` sites by up to 0.05 kg, a change for its own PR. Copy that reads `whoWfaZ` states no centile (V-M-287-2).
- `paraWeightPlausible` moves to `whoWfaZ` — this closes #162. **Interim (#287, Kael V-K-287-1, Maren V-M-287-3):** the exact flag sits 0.3–1.7 kg higher than the old approximate one at 3–24 months, so until the chip's confirm state ships (PR 4) a weigh-in at ≤ 24 months must also pass the old approximate ceiling (z ≤ +5 from (P97 − P50)/1.881); PR 1 never widens what is dosed at ≤ 24 months (past 24 months the exact flag admits more by design — the #162 fix, decision E — the pre-#161 typo exposure PR 4 closes). Removed in PR 4; the build warns from 2027-08-01 while it exists.
- New chip outcomes (#287): a weigh-in dated before the birth date → `baddate` ("dated before {poss} birth date"); a weigh-in past day 1826 → `outofrange`, calm copy: "The weight check here covers children up to age 5, so it can't work out a dose from {poss} weight. Ask your doctor or chemist for the dose." (Kael V-K-287-2, Maren V-M-287-4, Vela V-V-287-1/-2).
- **One age clock:** days / 30.4375 from `gvDaysBetween(dob, date)`; one boundary, 60.0 m = day 1826. An interpolation fraction > 1 fails closed.
- Placement: data.js beside `WHO_GROWTH` or core.js beside `GROWTH_VELOCITY_BANDS` — Carina's call.

### 4.2 The check (`gvWeightJumpCheck`, PR 2)
- **Pairs:** the `gvWeightWindow` pair, and the latest vs the immediately previous row when it differs. `confirm` if either is out of band. The anchor is the row `getLatestWeight()` returns, compared by date + wt. **Amended at build (#288, V-V-288-1 = V-M-288-1 = V-K-288-1):** rows are normalized first (the `gvMeasureRows` filter and order); any other row on the latest date with a different weight → `samedate`, and an equal one is a re-save, never a comparison; the pairs are taken from rows dated **before** the latest date — the `gvWeightWindow` pair, the latest earlier-dated row, and the latest earlier-dated row whose weight differs from the latest (the same typo on two dates, D5); an earlier date holding two different weights: every weight of that date is paired (V-CA-288-2). No earlier-dated row → `confirm`.
- **One total method predicate** over (start age, end age, gap), tested as a table:
  - end ≤ 24.0 m and gap ≤ 92 d → **velocity**: increment within [min(P1, 0), P99] of the row starting at floor(start age), clamped; gap ≤ 61 d uses the 2-month table, 62–92 the 3-month. Stated in the code: this deliberately differs from `gvBandAge` (the band containing the pair, not the centre of a rate).
  - start on or after day 365 of life (WHO's 12.0 m is day 365.25 — V-M-288-2), both ages ≤ 60.0 m, gap ≤ 184 d → **Δz**: z(latest at its age) − z(previous at its age) within [−kDown(gap), +kUp(gap)]; knots per sex at 61 / 91 / 122 / 183 d (girls +0.53/−0.54, +0.62/−0.60, +0.71/−0.66, +0.84/−0.81; boys +0.52/−0.54, +0.59/−0.59, +0.67/−0.64, +0.76/−0.77), linear in days between knots, ≤ 61 holds the 61 knot, 183 held to 184. The loss side never confirms a weight ≥ the previous weight.
  - anything else → **nocompare** → `confirm`.
- **Fail closed (PC-5):** written as `!(x >= lo && x <= hi)` → `confirm`. Missing row, unknown sex, non-finite gap, weight ≤ 0, age past 60.0 m, an implausible row in the pair, two different weights on one date (`samedate`), any exception → `confirm`. The loss guard never short-circuits the up test.
- **Seam (V-M-288-4, V-K-288-4):** at the knot gaps (61 / 91 / 183 d) the 24.0 m seam moves the limits by ≤ 15 g; at gaps under 61 d it steps — the gain ceiling tightens (girls 1197 → 794 g, the safe direction) and the loss floor widens (−318 → −729 g), so past 24 m a 5–6 % illness loss inside a week no longer reads `jump-down`. Within design (≤ 61 d holds the 61 knot); whether an illness-loss doctor line needs its own cited loss rule is a PR 4 question.
- Output: `{ verdict: ok|confirm, variant: jump-up|jump-down|nocompare|samedate|implausible|outofrange|error, method, pairs[], anchor }`. Also `reason` (`noprev` | `nosex` | `gap` | `age` | `norow` | `dob` — a row dated before the birth date), `badRow` naming only a weight that is off at an age on the table (never an age or date problem — Cipher #288 A1), and `pair` — the pair that decided, each pair carrying `changeG` and `badRow` (`a` | `b` | `both`) so a screen never re-derives the answer (V-V-288-2..5, V-K-288-2). When several pairs fail, the variant shown is ranked: samedate → outofrange → implausible → jump-up → jump-down → nocompare (the record first, then a jump, then "couldn't compare"). **Built in #161 PR 2:** `WHO_WT_VELOCITY` (2-/3-month rows, copied by script), `WHO_WT_VELOCITY_LONG` (the 4-/6-month rows ending at 24 m, transcribed from WHO's PDFs and checked against their own L/M/S) and `WHO_WT_DZ_KNOTS` (recomputed by `split/audit-who-velocity-v1.mjs` each build); not wired to the chip until PR 4.
- Advisory build warning from 2030-06-01 while no source past 60 m is registered.

### 4.3 Confirm persistence (`KEYS.growthConfirm`)
- `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': { '<grams>': [kinds] } }` — keyed by date **and** weight (grams, so no '.' reaches a Firestore field path), with what the check had asked: `calm` (couldn't compare), `amber` (a jump, a drop, an earlier weight that looks off; covers calm) or `samedate` (two different weights on one date). A confirm never writes the growth array (Carina: the array is replaced whole and the recovery push merges same-date rows local-wins, so a confirm on the row could resurrect a fixed typo already confirmed).
- Confirmed only for what the parent was shown: the stored kinds must cover the current ask (`gvWeightNeedsConfirm`), so a calm confirm never covers a jump that later evidence reveals; after a confirmed same-date conflict the jump test still runs against the earlier weigh-ins (`_gvJumpAfterSamedate`). A retype records only the question on screen (`data-arg3`); if the check changed since render, nothing is recorded and the card asks again.
- One merge (`gvConfirmMerge`) in `_syncMergeForPush`, `mergeOnReceive` and push-back: a plain union by date, weight and kind — no conflict, no lossy pick, never undone by the other phone. Receive pushes back what the cloud lacks; a phone's own confirms are cleared at a true first join. Not persist-defaults. Armed: `KEYS`, `SYNC_KEYS` (the `growth` doc), `SYNC_RENDER_DEPS` (fever card + Home's dose alert), the global accessors; export/reset via `KEYS`.
- The handler binds to the **displayed** date + weight (`data-arg`, `data-arg2`); finds the row by date + wt equality; writes nothing if no row matches. Accepted residuals: a phone whose growth is dirty for another reason can bring its own confirmed typo row back (two coinciding events); with no tombstones a confirm for a date + weight is permanent, so a fixed typo re-entered identically on the same date is already confirmed.

## 5. The leftover-risk flow (decision C; PR 4)
### 5.1 Reference weight
- **D** = the 24 h total ÷ the dosing weight. **R** = the 24 h total ÷ **W_ref** (R ≥ D).
- W_ref = the latest weight if confirmed by retyping or with no earlier weigh-in within 184 d; otherwise min(latest, every earlier in-window weigh-in **projected along its own weight-for-age z** to the latest date). No sex → min(latest, previous). Non-finite → min(latest, previous) → else load unknown.

### 5.2 Dose amounts
- One shared parser: comma decimal, ml / mg, ranges. Each dose → [lo, hi] mg: mg exact; ml + bottle exact; ml with no bottle ×24..×100; unreadable → [0, ∞]. Paracetamol only (word list incl. combination products; a renamed ibuprofen excluded).
- Total: over if lo-sum crosses; under only if hi-sum doesn't; else unknown. **Escalate on lo only;** a hi-only crossing → verdict `check` + "Which bottle…?" (V-M-C161F-6). Unknown never clears.
- Reads `load(KEYS.feverEpisodes)` fresh; an active paracetamol-named med in `KEYS.meds` → load unknown, reason named.

### 5.3 The rules (ratified)
Tiers: calm / amber / red ("Call your doctor today", the NHS 111 urgent-advice equivalent — judgment) / call now.
- **R0 dose beyond the packet:** logged < 4 h after the previous dose, or the 5th+ in the rolling 24 h → **red**; never call-now on its own; the higher of R0b / R1 wins and folds R0's line in; runs with no weight; never blocks logging. Copy (a): "This dose was {h} h {m} min after the last one. Doses close together happen, especially at night. NHS advice is to check with a doctor whenever a dose goes beyond what the packet says. Call your doctor today and tell them: {n} doses, {mg} mg in the last 24 hours, the last at {time}. Give no more paracetamol until {nextTime}." Copy (b): "That's {n} doses in 24 hours. The packet allows 4. …" {nextTime} = the later of 4 h after the last dose and the oldest of 4 leaving the window.
- **R0b one dose bigger than one dose:** lo > the chip's top dose for W_ref + one 0.1 ml step → red; lo ≥ 2× the chip top → call now (2× = judgment). "That dose is bigger than one dose for {wt} kg: {mg} mg, where the most for one dose is {top} mg. Call your doctor {today|now} and tell them the amount and the bottle. Give no more paracetamol until they say."
- **R1 rolling 24 h:** R ≤ 60 calm ("Last 24 h: {n} doses, {mg} mg."); 60 < D < 75 amber ("That's more than the usual most for a day for {wt} kg. Give no more paracetamol until {time}."); **D ≤ 60 < R → amber weight question**, verdict `check` ("These doses fit {pos} newest weight, {wt} kg. But {wt} kg is more than {pos} weigh-in on {date} ({prev} kg) would suggest. If {wt} kg is too high, the last 24 hours are over the usual most for a day. Check the weight before the next dose: weigh {obj} again, or check the number." chips "{wt} kg is right" → retype confirm, "Fix the weight / Add a weight"); D ≥ 75 → call now; R ≥ 75 > D → red + the weight question.
- **R2 more than 48 hours (RCH):** Kael's sweep over the 72 h alert window — the longest stretch with the rolling-24 h total on W_ref above 60: met > 48 h / maybe (only on hi) / no. Met only on R → the weight question first. With a GI sign in 48 h (vomit / projectile / bile, or new "Tummy pain" / "Seems sick" chips on `ep.actions`) → call now: "… Call your doctor now. {Pron} may need a blood test. Tell them: {n} doses, {mg} mg in the last 48 hours." - **R2b more than 3 days (NHS):** a dose on each of 4 consecutive local dates → amber: "This is the {4th} day of paracetamol. NHS advice is to speak to a doctor or pharmacist before giving it for more than 3 days."
- **R3 weigh again:** fever > 48 h or active vomiting / diarrhoea, a dose in 24 h, latest weigh-in predates the illness → amber: "If you can, weigh {obj} again before the next dose." + "Add a weight".
- Extra line: "Check other medicines' labels. Some cold syrups also contain paracetamol."

## 5A. PR 3 — illness-episode sync (design, 2026-11-04)
Consults: Carina (Foundation, V-CA-P3-1..14) and Kael (engine, V-K-P3-1..18), both concur-with-amendments; Maren on the late-entry rule (V-M-P3). Where they differed, the safer option was taken and is marked.

**Problem.** The four episode keys (fever, diarrhoea, vomiting, cold) are single-doc arrays. A receive saves the key but the module arrays (`_feverEpisodes` …) are hydrated once at load, so the other phone's dose is invisible until reload; and every write sends the whole array, so a phone that had not seen the other parent's dose overwrites it (BUGS P1, Kael C161C-8). The leftover-risk flow (§5) cannot be trusted until this is fixed.

**Design.**
1. **Entry ids** (V-CA-P3-1, V-K-P3-1). Every nested entry (readings, doses, actions, stools, fluids, wetDiapers, vomit `episodes`) gets an `id` at creation that edits never change. Legacy entries get a deterministic id `L:<arrayName>:<time>#<hash>` (an FNV hash of the entry's stable JSON, plus `.k` for the k-th identical copy — not a position, so phones holding different same-time sets name an entry alike; Kael V-K-289-6), assigned in memory by a lazy, idempotent `_epNormalize` at load and on both merge inputs; never written back on load. Cold `dailyLogs` keep `date` as identity; the later `time` wins. Time is not identity: edits rebuild it to the minute, and picker entries share minutes.
2. **One pure merge, both directions** (`_epMergeLists(kind, a, b)`): (1) union episodes by id; (2) drop ids in any `absorbed` list; (3) union each nested array by entry id; (4) tombstones `ep._del = [{ arr, id, at }]` — **the later action wins**: an entry edited after the delete survives (Kael V-K-P3-9; chosen over "delete always wins" because losing a corrected dose undercounts the 4-in-24 limit); (5) an entry present on both sides with different content: the later `editedAt` wins **whole** (never field by field — a removed `form` stays removed); with no `editedAt`, both are kept (a duplicate over-counts, the safe direction); (6) sort — episodes by (startedAt, id), entries by (time, id), cold logs by (date, time); (7) **status derived**: resolved iff `resolvedAt` is set and no symptom entry is later than `resolvedAt` (to the minute) — **chosen over "resolved always wins"** (Kael V-K-P3-2): a wrongly-active card costs one tap, a wrongly-resolved one silently stops the countdown, escalations and the dose gate; symptom entries per Maren's ruling (V-M-P3); an overridden resolve is kept as `resolveSuperseded`, and the reopened card says so — "Marked better on …, but new symptoms were logged after that, so this is open again" (Maren V-M-P3-7); **a newer same-kind episode started after the resolve keeps the old one resolved and takes its later entries** (Maren V-M-P3-5, BLOCKING) — the late entries belong to the illness the parent is tracking now. "Later" means after the minute Resolve was first pressed (`max(resolvedAt, resolvedAtOriginal)`), not after an end time edited earlier: entries logged before that press were logged while the episode was active (Cipher #289 A1) — except a red flag later than the edited end, which only a late-arriving entry can be and which moves too (Maren V-M-289-16); `resolvedAtOriginal` travels with the resolve group (V-M-289-18); the resolve group (resolvedAt, resolvedNotes, resolvedAtOriginal, resolvedEditedAt) comes from the side with the later `resolvedUpdatedAt`, stamped on resolve and on every save of the resolution sheet; (8) **fold concurrent actives** (V-CA-P3-6, V-K-P3-3): per kind, if more than one episode is active the survivor is the earliest (startedAt, id); the others' entries and tombstones move into it and their ids join `survivor.absorbed`; (9) `peakTemp` recomputed from readings (keep the larger finite side value if none); (10) `_epPrune`: keep every active episode, every fever episode with a dose in the last 24 h, then the newest up to 20, by (startedAt, id). Inputs are deep-cloned; the merge is commutative and idempotent (self-tested).
3. **Receive.** The four arrays are `SYNC_RENDER_DEPS` globals (`_syncSetGlobal` / `_syncGetGlobal`), with `mergeOnReceive` → `_epMergeLists`, `pushBackOnDiverge` (divergence: the stored merge vs the canonical remote, both through `_epStable`, which ignores the `__sync_*` stamps), the `render*History` renderers, and a post-receive hook that starts the fever check countdown when the other phone started an episode (V-K-P3-8). A merge that throws keeps local and skips the save — never last-write-wins (V-CA-P3-9).
4. **Push** (V-CA-P3-7). Every write to the `episodes` collection is a Firestore transaction: read the doc, merge each dirty key with `_epMergeLists`, write the merged value, set the shadow to it; contention or offline → `_syncMarkNeedsFull`. `_syncMergeForPush` routes the four keys to the same merge.
5. **Joining devices never seed** (V-CA-P3-8): while a join-pending flag is set, the first apply per episode key takes the remote copy outright, with no push-back.
6. **Edit sheets by id** (V-CA-P3-2, V-K-P3-5). Rows carry the entry id; onSave / onDelete re-find the live episode and entry by id at commit; gone → "This entry changed on the other phone. Close and reopen." The resolution sheet re-reads its list by id inside onSave.
7. **Deferred from the design round:** late entries that do not reopen an episode (a normal reading, a soft stool, a spit-up logged after the resolve) stay in its history without a "logged after it was marked better" label (Maren V-M-P3-8, DEFERRABLE); Carina V-CA-P3-D1..D4 and Kael V-K-P3-10..18 as listed in their returns.
8. **Not persist-defaults / always-populated** (V-CA-P3-13). Mixed-version households (an old-build phone without tombstones or merges) are a release note now and a minimum-build gate later (V-CA-P3-D1, V-K-P3-18).

## 6. Accepted residual
Worst true load that passes the check (four top doses, a typo at the band edge, real weight = previous, z0 −2..+2): ≤ 24 m velocity arm ≤ 72.8 (girls) / 72.0 (boys) mg/kg/day; Δz from 12 m up to 184 d ≤ 77.1 / 74.4; from 24 m ≤ 75.1 / 72.5. Below 75 is not guaranteed past ~157 d for girls. These cases read D = 60, R ≈ 65–67 → the R1 weight question: the next dose is held until the weight is checked. The case that survives is the same wrong number retyped at the confirm. References: BNF/TOXBASE 75 mg/kg/24 h; RCH > 200 mg/kg/24 h; RCH's > 48 h-with-GI criterion is met at > 60, which R2 catches.

- **Chain-of-trust residual (Cipher #288 finding 8):** a second typo close to a first (e.g. 9.5 → 15.8 → 15.9 with no weigh-in 28+ days back) is compared only with the first and can pass. Closed for new rows by the PR 4 confirm and the PR 5 entry check; legacy and synced rows stay exposed.
