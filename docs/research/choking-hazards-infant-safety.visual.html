The hazard list & how to cut it safe

The two killer shapes: round (corks the airway) and coin/cylindrical (windpipe-width). Modify, don't ban.

FoodMake it safe
Grapes / cherry tomatoesQuarter LENGTHWISE (halving is not enough)
Hot dogs / sausagesCut LENGTHWISE into strips, never coins
Nuts / groundnutsGrind/flake, or smooth nut butter thinned/spread thin
Hard raw carrot / appleCook soft, grate, or slice very thin
Popcorn, hard candy, marshmallow, gum, whole seedsDelay — can't reshape safe (under ~5)
Cheese / meat chunks · raisinsThin strips · chop small

General rule: pieces ≤½ inch (~1.25 cm) / a child's fingernail; soft enough to squash; round foods de-rounded. (AAP/CDC/NHS/HSE.)

⚑ Age window (honest split): AAP keeps high-risk round/firm foods until 4+ (development-dependent); NHS/HSE say no whole nuts or popcorn under 5. The app uses chokingUntilYears: 5 (conservative). A child has no grinding molars until ~16–29 months.

Gagging vs choking — the key distinction

GAGGING — normal & protective
LOUD · can breathe · strong cough
A normal reflex while learning to eat. Let it resolve — don't intervene.
CHOKING — emergency
QUIET · can't breathe · weak/no cough
Silent and fast. Act now — shout for help, start back blows.
⚑ Darker (Indian) skin: look for blue on the gums, inside the lips, or nailbeds — not skin colour. (NHS Best Start in Life.)
Why it matters: many parents intervene on a normal (frequent) gag when they shouldn't — and may miss the silent real thing. Teaching this distinction is the single highest-value piece of choking parent-education.

Choking first aid — the emergency floor

This is mechanical airway rescue — NOT anaphylaxis. No adrenaline, no drugs.
Effective cough → encourage coughing
If they can cough forcefully, let them — don't intervene.
Baby <1, silent/can't breathe → 5 back blows + 5 chest thrusts
Face-down, head low, heel of hand between shoulder blades ×5; then face-up, 2 fingers mid-chest below nipple line ×5. Alternate, repeat. NEVER abdominal thrusts under 1.
Child >1 → 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts
No blind finger sweep (pushes it deeper). Don't dangle by the ankles.
Not clearing → call 112 / 108 · unresponsive → CPR
Call early (speakerphone, hands free) and start CPR if they become unresponsive.
⚑ Flag: an RCUK 2025 update reportedly moves infant chest thrusts to a "thumb-encircling" technique — unverified this pass (PDF unreadable); use the established two-finger wording; the core 5+5 is unaffected.

Indian context & consensus

INDIAN CORE — verified
Groundnut = #1 aspirated food
Pondicherry 67.5% · Belgaum 27.3% · Eastern India 31.8%. Handed loose to toddlers.
SUPARI / areca nut
Double danger — in airways AND toxic
Seizures + liver injury on ingestion. Keep away from children entirely.
