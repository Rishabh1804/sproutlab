# Recipe research — authoritative baby-food sources (deep-research, 2026-06-03)

Provenance for the curated recipes in `gen-hero.mjs`. Two parallel deep-research
passes (6–9 m first-foods/purées; 8–12 m Indian combination meals), each
cross-verified against ≥2 authoritative sources. Ziva context: ~9 months,
mostly vegetarian, eggs/fish possible → **8–9 m band: mashed/lumpy + soft
finger foods.**

## Authoritative framework
| Parameter | 6–8 m | 9–11 m |
|---|---|---|
| Energy from complementary food/day | ~200 kcal | ~300 kcal |
| Meals/day | 2–3 | 3–4 + 1–2 snacks |
| Amount per meal | 2–3 Tbsp → up | ~½ cup (125 ml) |
| Texture | thick purée/mash (stays on spoon) | mashed + lumpy + finger foods |
| Energy density | ≥0.8 kcal/g — add ¼–½ tsp ghee/oil (1–2 g) | same |
| Cereal:pulse weight ratio (combos) | ~1.2–1.5 : 1 (IAP "cereal + pulse" formula) | |

Sources: WHO 2023 [NBK596423] · WHO/PAHO [NBK148957] · IAP Ch-040 · ICMR-NIN
DGI-2024 · NHS Start4Life · FAO am866e · AAP HealthyChildren · CDC.

## Safety rules (each cross-verified ≥2 sources) — baked into the tagline composer
| Rule | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Honey** | NONE < 12 m (infant botulism). Hard cutoff. → composer **strict lead**. |
| **Added salt** | None < 12 m (< 1 g salt/day); immature kidneys. |
| **Added sugar / juice** | None < 12 m (no juice < 12 m, free sugar < 2 y). Fruit sweetens. |
| **Allergens (egg, peanut, nuts, dairy, sesame, fish)** | Introduce EARLY (~6 m), **one at a time**, watch ≥1 day. |
| **Egg** | Fully cooked (firm white + yolk). |
| **Nuts (almond/walnut/peanut)** | NEVER whole — ground fine / smooth paste only (choking). → composer **fold** "ground". |
| **Grapes / round foods** | Halved/quartered. → composer **fold** "halved". |
| **Cow's milk** | OK in cooking from 6 m; NOT a main drink until 12 m. |
| **Hard veg/fruit (carrot, apple, potato)** | Cook until soft. |
| **Nitrates (spinach/beet/carrot)** | Concern only < 6 m — safe home-cooked at 9 m. |

⚠ **Flagged disagreement:** IAP Table 1 permits "some sugar/jaggery" for energy
density; **WHO 2023 + NHS say no added sugar.** Resolution adopted: follow the
stricter international line — **fruit (banana/date/apple) for sweetness, no
added sugar/jaggery** for a well-fed baby.
