The Compass vs. the North Star

Synthesis · 2026-07-11 · Two distinct layers to every Companion spec.

North star → fixed

Who a Companion is and where they're going. Persona, voice, jurisdiction, naming-lore. Held across every refresh. It does not move.

Compass → accretes

How a Companion navigates. Bearings earned in session, kept as standing judgment for when intention alone won't say which way to turn. It accumulates.

The convention

  • Section ## Compass — session-earned bearings, placed before ## References.
  • A shared intro paragraph (verbatim) frames north-star vs compass.
  • Each bearing: bold lead-in (date, source) + 1–2 sentences in the Companion's voice. Dated + sourced always.
  • The same section in both modes (agent + skill) — judgment is invocation-agnostic.
  • Scribes exempt — voiceless at birth, they hold no standing judgment. A compass is for those who navigate; Scribes are navigated.

Second pattern — the north star can move

SproutLab's north star was re-set this session (“cozy nursery journal” → “a health intelligence system with the soul of a journal, evolving with the child”). Fixed within a session; amendable between sessions by ratified decision. When it moved, warmth was promoted (brief → law), not dropped.
