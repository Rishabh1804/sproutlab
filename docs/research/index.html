Honey Critical · infant botulism

No honey before 12 months, in any form — the danger is infant botulism, not nutrition. Cooking/baking does not make it safe. Treatable but a medical emergency. For an Indian family, the ghutti / prelacteal-feed tradition is the live vector.

How this is built

One data spine — food-effects.manifest.js — holds a summary record per food, feeding both this research layer and (eventually) the in-app surfacing. New foods are authored from _TEMPLATE.food-dashboard.html. Once enough foods exist, a unified hub will let you browse one food, compare several, or slice by reaction. See README.md for the full plan.

Three depth layers per food: visual dashboard (skim) → long-form (full prose) → .md (source of truth). All carry the same verified figures.
