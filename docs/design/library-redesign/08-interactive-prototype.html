Honey can carry spores that cause infant botulism in babies under 1. A baby's gut can't handle them yet. This includes honey cooked or baked into food.

The rule
No honey at all before 12 months
If honey was given
  • Watch over the next days for constipation, weak cry, poor feeding, floppiness

Offering the common allergens from around 6 months — and keeping them in the diet — markedly lowers the chance of a food allergy. Waiting does not help.

The priority allergens
EggPeanutTree nutsFishSesameSoyWheatDairy
How to do it

One new allergen at a time, on a calm day, earlier in the day. Once it's tolerated, keep it in ~2× a week — regular exposure is what protects. Always in a smooth, age-safe form.

Know the reaction signs
  • Hives spreading, swelling of lips/face, wheeze or trouble breathing, repeated vomiting, floppiness

Call 112 and use a prescribed adrenaline auto-injector if you have one.

Whole cow's milk can become a main drink. No sugary drinks or juice before then (or after, ideally).

  • ~6 moSmooth purées & soft single foods. Start iron-rich foods, and begin introducing the allergens.

In an emergency
Choking
Recognise

Silent or weak cough, can't breathe / cry / make noise, turning blue, going limp.

Do

A mechanical emergency — NO adrenaline. Alternate 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts. Never abdominal thrusts (Heimlich) under 1. Call 112/108. If unresponsive, start CPR.

Allergic reaction (anaphylaxis)
Recognise

Hives spreading, swelling of lips / face / tongue, wheeze or trouble breathing, repeated vomiting, sudden floppiness.

Do

Call 112 right away and use a prescribed adrenaline auto-injector if you have one. An antihistamine does not treat anaphylaxis.

Delayed vomiting (FPIES)
Recognise

Repeated forceful vomiting 1–4 hours after a food, with paleness, lethargy or floppiness — no rash.

Do

Seek urgent care for fluids — babies can dehydrate fast. Note the food and time; mention FPIES.

Suspected poisoning / botulism
Recognise

After honey or an unsafe food: constipation, weak cry, poor feeding, drooping, floppiness.

Do

Seek medical care promptly and say what was eaten and when. Keep the packaging if any.
