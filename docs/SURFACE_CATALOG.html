grapes — cut as shown in "Make it safe to chew" below.
Serving for Ziva (14 mo): ½ medium katori (≈ 50–75 g fruit). A toddler snack is about ½ cup (≈ 8 tbsp) — a guide, not a target; let her appetite lead. ICMR-NIN · WHO/PAHO · IAP
  1. QUARTER grapes — never whole, and halving is not enough
  2. Ripe, soft fruit only; peel and seed
  1. Dice ripe papaya and banana into soft, small pieces.
  2. Quarter each grape lengthways (cut into 4).
  3. Serve in a bowl for her to pick up with her fingers.
Ripe papaya is vitamin-A rich
Whole fruit instead of juice — WHO says limit 100% juice
Good practice for her pincer grasp
No chaat masala or salt
No sugar or honey drizzle
Always seated and supervised while eating
grapes — Choking hazards — cut it to make it safe; whole forms wait until ~5 The hazard is the form. Whole nuts and popcorn are not for a child under 5; high-risk round or firm foods wait until about 4 to 5 — the app uses 5 to be safe. Whole peanut and tree nut have their OWN records — this covers the rest of the choking set.
If this happens, it's an emergency
  • a silent or weak cough; can't breathe, cry, or make noise
  • turning blue (check the gums, inside the lips, or nailbeds on darker skin)
  • clutching the throat, distress, or going limp
Watch for
  • gagging — LOUD, normal, and protective: let it resolve, don't intervene
  • an effective, forceful cough: encourage the cough, don't intervene

Choking is a MECHANICAL emergency — not an allergy, so NO adrenaline. If the cough is effective, encourage coughing. If the cough is silent or she can't breathe — alternate 5 back blows (face-down across your lap, head low, or leaning forward; heel of the hand between the shoulder blades) and 5 abdominal thrusts (from behind, fist between the belly button and the ribs, pull sharply inwards and upwards; keep off the lower ribs). No blind finger sweep — remove only what you can clearly see. Not clearing → call 112 / 108; if she becomes unresponsive → start CPR. Even once it clears, get her checked — abdominal thrusts can injure inside.
