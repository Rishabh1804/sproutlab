Domain Score hero (per-tab)

Per-tab score hero. Rendered inside its real .card.card-hero.hero-{domain} shell (the floor’s two-domain semantic gradient + the 5px ::before accent stripe) wrapping .domain-score-hero.dsh-{domain}. The RING carries the score-band polarity (.dsh-ring-{label}), the card-hero gradient carries domain identity — the two never compete (§Polarity Collision). Component bars .dcb-{high|mid|low}. Components, weights and detail strings mirror _spGetDomainDefs (diet.js); details show in full and wrap (no truncation). Pills are one column at ≤400px and at medium/large zoom — the ≤400px rule follows the browser window, so narrow this page to see it.
