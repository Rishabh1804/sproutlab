Ragi Banana Porridge
Ingredients
Ragi6m+1 tbsp
Banana6m+¼ ripe
Gheeok½ tsp
Method
  1. Dry-roast ragi 2–3 min until aromatic, then whisk into water and cook, stirring, 5–8 min to a smooth porridge.
  2. Mash ¼ ripe banana and stir in off the heat.
  3. Finish with ½ tsp ghee. Smooth at 6–8m; soft lumps at 8–10m.
Good to know
Do
  • Pair iron with a vitamin-C fruit
  • Start with a small quantity
Don't
  • No honey/sugar under 1
  • Don't store mashed banana
Source: ICMR-NIN — Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024
