In an emergency
Peanut
Severe allergic reaction · anaphylaxis
Do now
1If you have an adrenaline auto-injector — use it now. Outer thigh, hold 10 seconds. (Most families won't have one — if not, go straight to step 2.)
2Call 112 — tell them your baby has trouble breathing after food; say “anaphylaxis” if you can.
3Lie her flat, legs raised. Hard to breathe → let her sit up. Vomiting → on her side. Never stand her up.
4No better after 5 minutes? A second dose, if you have one.
Call 112
Recognise — any one
  • trouble breathing or wheezing
  • swelling of the face, lips, or tongue
  • going floppy, pale, or very sleepy
Choking on it instead? See the Choking card.
After
  • Go to hospital even if she settles — a reaction can return hours later.
  • Note the time, the food, and what you gave.
