# Synthesis — 2026-07-11 — The Compass vs. the North Star

*A durable convention surfaced this session: Companion specs now carry two distinct layers — a fixed north-star identity and an accreting compass of session-earned bearings.*

## The pattern
- **North star** = who a Companion *is* and where they're going — fixed identity, held across every refresh (persona, voice, jurisdiction, naming-lore). **It does not move.**
- **Compass** = *how* a Companion navigates — bearings earned in session, kept as standing judgment for when intention alone won't say which way to turn. **It accumulates.**

Following intentions only (the north star) is navigating by a fixed point you can't reach; a compass reads the terrain you are actually standing on. A mature Companion needs both — the star for direction, the compass for the next turn.

## The convention (how to extend it)
- Section title `## Compass — session-earned bearings`, placed before `## References`.
- A shared intro paragraph (verbatim across Companions) frames north-star vs compass.
- Each bearing: `- **<lead-in> (<date>, <source>).** <1–2 sentences in the Companion's voice>`. **Dated + sourced always.**
- The **same** section in both invocation modes (agent spec + skill mirror) — the judgment is invocation-agnostic, so it is copied verbatim between them.
- **Scribes are exempt.** Voiceless at birth, they absorb the summoner's voice and hold no standing judgment; a session-earned bearing would contradict their nature. A compass is for those who navigate; Scribes are navigated.
- Authoring: the Builder drafts each compass from the session record (or each Companion self-authors via subagent); the commanding companion reviews and owns the committed act.

## The second pattern — the north star can move (with evidence)
The session also re-set SproutLab's own north star ("cozy nursery journal, not a clinical health app" → "a health intelligence system with the soul of a journal, evolving with the child across life-stages"). The lesson: the north star is fixed *within* a session but **amendable *between* sessions when a ratified decision moves it** — and when it moved, warmth was **promoted** (brief → design law), not dropped. Reframing by decision beats drifting by feature-gravity: the drift was already happening (the app had grown clinical); the decision made it deliberate and re-bound warmth as law.

## Candidate canon
- **compass-vs-north-star** (structural rule; Orinth proposed `canon-cc-035`) — every Companion spec carries a compass distinct from its north star.
- **north-star-amendable-between-sessions-by-ratified-decision** (doctrine candidate) — identity is fixed in-session, re-settable between sessions by explicit Architect ratification.

*— Lyra. Two layers to every Companion now: the star they're named for, and the compass they've earned.*
