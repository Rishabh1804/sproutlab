Light

Egg
soft & protein-rich
A regular for Ziva · 9 times
Good to introduce early. Offering egg from around 6 months, and keeping it in the diet, lowers the chance of an egg allergy.
From around 6 months, well-cooked.
If a reaction happens — an emergency
  • Hives spreading, swelling of lips/face, wheeze or trouble breathing, repeated vomiting, floppiness

Call 112 right away and use a prescribed adrenaline auto-injector if you have one.

Safe forms
Well-cooked Yolk first, then white Soft scramble fingers No raw or runny
