Mercury & species — the new axis

Relative mercury (higher bar = more mercury = worse for a baby). Large predators accumulate methylmercury, a neurotoxin for the developing brain.

Bars are relative, not absolute ppm. Avoid (FDA/EPA): shark, swordfish, king mackerel, marlin, bigeye tuna, orange roughy, Gulf tilefish. Limit: albacore/yellowfin tuna (1 serving/wk). Best: salmon, sardine, bangda (Indian mackerel), rohu, pomfret, trout, canned light tuna.

AVOID (high-mercury)

⚑ Indian-authority hook (FSSAI): Fish 0.5 mg/kg mercury but predatory fish (tuna/swordfish/shark/marlin) 1.0 mg/kg — FSSAI itself codes those as higher-mercury. A food-safety ceiling, not an infant directive.
⚑ Serving: children 2 servings/week from Best Choices; ages 1–3 ≈ 1 oz (~28g). No FDA figure under age 1 — the list logic still applies.

Safe prep & the benefit

Three musts before serving

Debone. NHS: "Remove all bones from meat or fish." Flake and check by hand — rohu/katla are very bony (~104/108 pin bones).
Cook through. No raw fish/sushi, no raw/lightly-cooked shellfish for a baby. Cold-smoked fish: cook to steaming.
Low salt. Under-1s need <1g salt/day — skip dried/salted fish (Bombay duck/bombil) and salty curry.
The benefit (why it's worth it): oily fish is a top source of long-chain omega-3 (DHA) — builds nerve cells in the brain and eyes — plus protein, vitamin D and iron. NHS: "the health benefits are greater than the risks." Aim ~2 fish portions/week including 1 oily.
⚑ Girl-specific (Ziva): NHS caps oily fish at ≤2 portions/week for girls (4 for boys) because oily-fish pollutants can build up. Iodine, in Indian IYCF, comes from iodized salt — not fish.

Reaction floor & Indian context

Fish allergy tends to be lifelong (unlike egg/milk), and finfish is a separate allergen from shellfish. Watch ~2 hours after a first taste.

Mild / one system — stop, monitor, call doctor
hives/rash · swelling (lips, face, eyes) · itchy mouth · vomiting
Severe / anaphylaxis — EMERGENCY (any one)
trouble/noisy breathing · tongue or throat swelling · hoarse cry · pale and floppy
Act: lay flat → adrenaline → call 112 / 108
Antihistamine does NOT treat anaphylaxis. In some sensitive people, cooking steam can trigger a reaction.
