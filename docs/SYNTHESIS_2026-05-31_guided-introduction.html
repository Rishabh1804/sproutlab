The one idea

A food-effect's polarity (encourage vs warn) and gate direction (a soft "now is good" floor vs a hard "blocked below" ceiling) are data, not code. Honey taught the layer one shape — hazard → avoid → watch → seek-care. That shape is right for an acute toxin with no upside, and wrong for nuts, egg, seeds, and milk, where the safe action is the opposite of avoidance and fear-framing causes real harm.

A foodClass taxonomy makes the shape a property of the food — and lets one food carry more than one (peanut is both allergen · introduce early and choking · by form).

The polarity flip — honey vs the staples

● Acute-toxin model (honey)
  • Polarity: warn / avoid
  • Gate: hard ceiling — blocked below 12mo
  • Hazards: one (botulism)
  • Chrome: rose
  • Right action: do not give
● Guided-introduction model (peanut, tree nut…)
  • Polarity: encourage
  • Gate: soft floor — "now is good" from ~6mo
  • Hazards: two (allergy + choking-by-form)
  • Chrome: sage benefit, amber caution
  • Right action: introduce early, in safe form, keep offering

The spine both Governors converged on — the emergency floor never moves

⚑ Invariant (Maren A-1 · Vela V-1/V-3)

Benefit may lead the card. The severe-reaction signs + the emergency action render unconditionally, non-collapsibly, above the fold. "Calm secondary" governs visual weight and tone — not presence or reachability.

Encoded as: a severeSigns[] field distinct from the calm mild watchFor[]; an amber strip (serious-without-alarm — rose stays reserved for acute-toxin); never inside an accordion; guarded by e2e. The .cons-severe strip shipped in #184.

What the Governor chain caught that nothing else would

Two safety-tier bugs, both invisible to build + e2e + automated review, both caught by Maren's lens — "what if this data is wrong and a parent acts on it?"

soya (#182)
\bsoy\b doesn't match "soya" — the dominant Indian spelling — so logged "soya chunks" silently lost its allergen flag. Domain knowledge, not a code smell.
V-M-206 — the session's signature catch (#184)
"Whole almond" / "whole cashew" resolved to the 6-month introduce-early floor instead of the 60-month whole-nut choking gate — silently downgrading protection for the canonical infant choking hazard. The fix routes a named whole/chopped nut on a choking-by-form food to the 60mo gate. Verified 12/12; Cipher hammered every boundary. Build + e2e were green; only a Care lens caught it.
